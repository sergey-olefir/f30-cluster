1 · The bottom row, 1:1

The full 1920 px panel width, cropped to the bottom row band (y 655–720 of the cluster). Rendering A. Scroll sideways if the window is narrower than the cluster.

BMW's own street-name field (outlined) sits at x 795–1126, y 669–709 (docs/POLISH-D-PROPOSAL.md). The shield is 40 px tall, so it fills that row's height exactly. The group is centred on x 960 and runs wider than BMW's 331 px field rather than shrinking the name — the owner's decision. The hard limit is the two dials: at the row's top edge (y 669, where they are widest) the clear span is x 581–1339, 757 px. The working budget is x 660–1260, 600 px, which leaves the worst case (T-17-19 + E 50, 226 px of shields and gaps) about 374 px of name, roughly 24 characters at 30 px.

2 · The E green: the standard beside ours

Both at the row's real scale on black, day and night, with the contrast of the number on the plate measured live (WCAG 2.x relative luminance). Our number is the theme's fg #F4F6FA, not pure white, so both figures are given.

The DSTU green is the brightest of the three hues, so white on it is weak — on the real sign too. Scaling it keeps the hue (155°) and the saturation (98 %) and only lowers the value, so it stays the E green rather than drifting to teal. At night both land on the same plate, so only the day plate changes.

4 · How the box grows

Width is W = 2·border + 2·pad + Σadvance(c) = 18 + ink, clamped to ≥ 44 and rounded up to an even number. Height is fixed at 40.
