## 1. What was built

(`sh tools/twin-dial/b6/bake_b6.sh`; `--check` writes nothing and fails on drift). Every atlas that was not changed is
byte-identical to `main`'s (the bake ran, `git status` showed only the files below).

The gear's own atlas on the car goes from 91 200 B (64 px, 912 x 50) to 149 240 B (+58 040 B), either way. The letters are
not "figures" for the engine's gpu text (their advance is not 82), so they go through its spare cells: every gear string the
engine's `gear` token can give (P R N; D S M with 1..8; D S M alone: 30 strings) needs at most 6 cells, the engine has 6
(worst "M1": 6) - checked in `bench/test-twin-fonts.js` with the engine's `_gpuGrid`. The cap height of `D M N P R S` is 63 px
(a digit with its overshoot is 65): SPEC-R3 2.7 says about 66; I did not bake the letters larger because a digit and a letter
of one gear string ("D3") must match.

**The sign's number.** The sign's white disc is 50.8 px across (outer r 33, ring 7.6). The Saira file has no width below 100,
so the two sign atlases squeeze the heaviest weight (700) across by 0.88 / 0.85 in the reduction. Measured: "88" in `td_sign` is
38 x 23 px, 3.2 px clear of the ring at the corners; "888" in `td_sign3` is 40 x 18 px, 3.5 px clear (test: at least 3 px).

**Budget.** Noto full-text atlases (limit 0.8 MiB each): `td_title` 0.45, `td_msg` 0.45, `td_row` 0.43, `td_val` 0.43,
`td_tabs` 0.32, `td_tab` 0.31, `td_body` 0.29, `td_sub` 0.24, `td_u21` 0.15, `td_u19` / `td_u20` 0.14 MiB. The font folder grew from
4.48 MB to 5.63 MB (+1.15 MB in `hud.pack`); the engine loads an atlas only when a widget uses it (`hud_text.loadFont`), so
memory follows use. Deleting `td_gear` later gives back 69 KB.

### 1.2 Pictograms (brief B6 item 2)

`design/twin-dial/vt/pictograms.js` is the source; `shared/twin_icons.js` (generated, Node 8 clean) and
`apps/twin_dial/hud/icons/icons.json` (slot table, ink boxes, alpha hashes) are regenerated by
`node tools/twin-dial/a1/build_twin_icons.js`. **83 icons** (was 25): the 25 of 0.1.0 unchanged, plus 58.

- **K2's 24 drawings** moved from `tools/twin-dial/mockup/pictos_r3.py` (`twin-r3/k2`; the spec says 25, the file has 24).
  23 are identical to K2's (compared on a 64 x 64 distance grid against the python data); `td_phone_none` is renamed
  **`td_phone_off`** (the spec's and the brief's name); `td_cruise` is **redrawn** (K2's was a speedometer on a base line;
  SPEC-R3 9 asks for "a speedometer with an arrow").
- **New, our own:** `td_proj` (the projection icon: a display on a foot with broadcast waves; generic, not any system's
  logo), `td_cruise` (speedometer with an arrowed needle), `td_cruise_acc` (the same over a car ahead, seen from behind, with a
  distance bar), `td_limiter` (LIM in a frame), `td_end_stripes` (five diagonal stripes clipped to the disc of the box; the
  sign's page paints the ring, the white disc and these stripes dark).
- **The manoeuvre set of `twin-r3/sn`'s `guidance.json` `pictograms_needed`:** the 9 turns (`td_straight`, `td_turn_{slight_,hard_,}{left,right}`,
  `td_uturn_{left,right}`), forks and exits (`td_fork_left/right/straight`, `td_exit_left/right`, `td_merge_left/right`), destination
  flags (`td_flag_left/centre/right`), lane changes (`td_lane_change_left/right`), the plain dot (`td_dot`) and the **roundabouts by
  exit for both driving directions: `td_rb1..6_r` (right-hand traffic, counter-clockwise) and `td_rb1..6_l` (mirrored)**. Not drawn:
  ferry and tunnel (`guidance.json` lists them under "other"; the brief's list does not).
- **Slot sizes (SPEC-R3 7 and the places that name them)**, in `icons.json`: tab 36 (the nine tab icons: `td_fuel cc_warn td_radio
  td_media td_proj td_phone td_phone_bars td_phone_off td_nav`), gauge 32, symbol 54 (the card's cell), row 32 (the tab-2 rows),
  page 54, big 66 (the media source pictogram, the call's handset, the empty page), cruise 36, sign 51, arrow 120 (the guidance
  page), lane 30 (the lane row and the navigation tab's turn arrow after P12), mark 32 (small marks: mute, muted microphone, play,
  pause and the other mockup symbols). The engine paints at the widget's own size, so a slot is the size a place uses and a record
  for the hash test, not a limit.
- **Hypotheses named:** the six roundabout exit angles (`RB_EXIT` = 96, 52, 8, -38, -84, -132 degrees from straight ahead; a drawing
  convention - the car's `exitAngle`, whose unit is unconfirmed, overrides it on the page that has it); the roundabout's exit number
  is drawn by the page inside the ring (a clear disc of about 0.17 of the box). The arrow stems run out of the bottom of the box by
  up to 0.08 (an arrow starts at the edge of its picture, as K2's turn arrows already did) and K2's `td_bars` has its tallest bar
  0.04 over the top; the test lists both exceptions, no other icon touches the box edge.
- **The sheet for the owner** (git-ignored): `bake/work/twin-dial/r3b/pub/B6/icons_1x.png` and `icons_4x.png` (every icon at its slot
  size and at four times, painted by the engine's own painter; labelled). Made by `tools/twin-dial/b6/icon_sheet.js` + `.py`.

## 5b. Open problems and honest limits

- No car: nothing here is proven on the panel. Sizes are from the SPEC; the sign's numerals' clearance and the gear's cell count are
  computed from the atlases and the engine's grid, not seen.
- The roundabout exit angles are our convention (hypothesis, above). The lane-row arrows are the same drawings at 30 px (a stroke 0.15 =
  4.5 px); whether they read in the car is for the car session.
- The Saira file's narrowest width is 100, so the sign's digits are squeezed by resampling, not drawn condensed; look at the sheet.
- The gear letters are 63 px tall against the digits' 65 (their overshoot); SPEC-R3's "about 66" is met by the digits.
- `hud.pack` grows by 1.15 MB; deleting `td_gear` (after B4's hunk) gives back 69 KB.
