## 1. Register: impersonal, like a technical paper

The owner asked for no "власник / owner", no story in the third person, impersonal constructions. The human / AI split
is now stated once, in "Коротко" / "In short": a team of AI agents (one lead, parallel package agents) under human
direction; the person set goals and rules, took decisions, photographed and pressed buttons in the car; the agents
measured, drew, wrote code, tests and reports. After that no person appears as a character.
