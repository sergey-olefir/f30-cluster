## Light direction (P1 and P9 depend on this)
- **Bezel ridge brightness against angle** (05, 04 and 06 agree; the ridge is fitted relative to itself, so decentring cancels out). The ridge shows two lobes on one axis:
  - Narrow lobe at **340 cw12 (math 110)**.
  - Broad lobe at **148 cw12 (math 302)**.
  - Dimmest at ~40 cw12, about 0.3 of the peak.
  - Fit: b 8 + 155·exp(8(cos(θ−340)−1)) + 228·exp(1(cos(θ−148)−1)), in L/235, rms 21.
  - 17 and 07 also show the left (~270 cw12) bright. This is added as a weak third lobe.
- **Highlight position across the tube**: the centroid and skew shift is < 0.002 R in 05, 04 and 03, below the resolution. The across-profile cannot tell which end of the axis holds the light. In 13 @4x (~82 cw12) the profile is: dark gap 0.955–0.961, grey inner flank 0.962–0.981 (0.15 of the ridge), dip 0.982, ridge peak 0.991 (σ_in 0.0048, σ_out 0.011), black by 1.015.
- **Groove inner lip (0.82 R)**: this is the decisive cue. It is lit on the upper-left side only, with a broad maximum at ~**318 cw12 (math 132)** fading monotonically to the lower right (04: 43–58 over 260–340, 10 at 82, 8 at 112; 05 the same with flare).
- **Conclusion**: the key light is at the upper left, math **~110–135, use 125**. The lower-right bezel lobe is that light's highlight on the tube's far flank (or a second light drawn on purpose). It is not a separate key.

## Other findings
- Rev scale: 0 rpm at 250.5 cw12, 32.05° per 1000 rpm (four photos, rms 0.5–0.75°). The theme's 250.5 / 32.1 is within the error.
- Ticks (13 @4x, at R 335):

  | Tick | r (R) | Width (FWHM) | Level |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Major | 0.8165–0.903 | 3.8→4.2 px | white |
  | Half | 0.853–0.903 | as wide as a major | white |
  | Minor | 0.866–0.903 | 2.2 px | 0.6 white |

  - The theme's majors (9 px wide) are about 2× too wide.
  - A light spot sits 0.014 R past the end of each bright tick: σ 0.011 along × 0.0095 across, 0.42 of white. The minor ticks have almost none.
  - The cool fringe at the outer ends looks like **lens chromatic aberration**: the numerals show blue on their outer edges and warm on their inner edges. It is kept faint.
- The tick ring sits about 0.025 R off the bezel centre toward ~235 cw12 in 04, 03 and 05. This is either parallax or photo geometry, and it dominates the 05 and 04 edge errors on the tick band (3.5–4 px). The fit to 13 is 1.2–1.6 px.
- OFF arc:
  - r 0.8265 (the inner end of the 0 rpm tick), from 232.7 to 250.5 cw12.
  - Half-width 0.0042 → 0.0032 R; level 1.0 → 0.8 toward OFF.
  - OFF mark at 233 cw12, r 0.82–0.905. It shows in 17 only (weak).
  - The needle parks at ~230 (dynamic).
- Red zone (05):
  - Bright band at r 0.896–0.914, (232, 20, 16).
  - Dim fill at r 0.866–0.896, red 25→75 toward the end.
  - Pad 0.55°, one dark gap at 7.2k; no minor ticks inside the zone.
  - The start rpm is a parameter. 17 shows the red over 0.825–0.875 instead.
- P box (05):
  - Bottom edge: an arc **concentric** with the dial at r 0.683, ±27.5° about 180. It is brightest at ±15° and fades by about 27°.
  - Top edge: flat at v 0.515, |u| < 0.145, corners rounded with r 0.035, brightest at u ≈ +0.025.
  - Sides: run to (±0.30, on the arc).
  - Fill: L ~9–10.
  - **car4 L (`docs/CAR4-L-REPORT.md`) re-measured the top, which read as a dome:** a flat shelf at v 0.515, |u| < 0.130, corners r 0.012; straight shoulders at 42° below the horizontal, meeting the arc at |u| ≈ 0.26 (the arc runs on past them to its ±27.5° ends, as on 05). The shelf line peaks at 0.9 of the arc at u ≈ +0.015 with s 0.093; the shoulders are at 0.15. The bottom arc and the fill are unchanged. The p-box row in the table below is P2's fit of the old shape.
- Disc rim at 0.484 R, with a brightness ramp inside it from 0.40 to 0.476. The inner ring is at 0.330 (weak). The floor line is at v 0.25. The disc, inner ring and rim all end at the floor line.
