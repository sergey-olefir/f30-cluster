# C7 (P5): signs and pictograms

Regenerate: `cd bake/src && $PY -m C7.build` (about 20 s). Code: `bake/src/C7/icons.py` (shapes -> SVG + 8x supersampled raster),
`bake/src/C7/build.py` (fit, QC, manifest). Measurement scripts: `bake/work/C7/m1.py` to `m11.py`. Build log with every number: `bake/work/C7/build_log.txt`.
Assets: `apps/f30_cluster/hud/baked/C7/*.albedo.png` plus SVG twins in `C7/svg/`. rsvg-convert renders of the SVGs match the PNGs
(alpha MAE 0.003-0.016, anti-aliasing only). Contact sheet: `bake/work/C7/contact_sheet_4x.png`.

All drawings are generic shapes made from measured proportions. Nothing is traced, and no font is used: the speed-limit figure is
live text, and only its box is given.

The pictograms miss the 1.0 px edge target. Their references are 15-25 px blobs, upsampled 2-2.4x and bloomed (17 has veiling glare), so the
crisp interior details (window, hose, ticks) cannot be resolved. The size and placement fits use NCC brute force (`fit_search` in the manifest).
The signs meet the target.

## Composition

The signs need independent visibility. That means either 2 image layers (125 312 bytes) or the existing single WBSIGN effect layer with the new
numbers (0 bytes, recommended). The manifest's `composition` lists the 2-PNG variant.

The pictograms get no layers of their own. Merge them into their hosts:
- fuel pump into the C3 albedo (P3);
- oil can or coolant into C4 (P3);
- battery into the C6 EfficientDynamics arc image (P4);
- range icon into the bottom-bar image (P4).

The warning triangle is check-control state: it needs a layer only if the theme draws its own check control.
