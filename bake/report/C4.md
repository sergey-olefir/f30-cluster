# P3: C4 (oil temperature mini gauge)

C4 is the mirror of C3, with a blue cold zone, a red hot zone and an oil-can pictogram. The full report for both gauges is in [`C3.md`](C3.md). The manifest is `bake/manifest/C4.json`, the assets are in `apps/f30_cluster/hud/baked/C4/`, and the generator is `cd bake/src && $PY -m C3.build && $PY -m C3.qc_run`.
