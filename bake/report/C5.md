# C5: centre window (P4, COMFORT)

Regenerate: `cd bake/src && UV_CACHE_DIR=../work/.uvcache uv run -q --no-project --with opencv-python-headless --with numpy --with pillow python -m C6.build --all`
(builds C5 + C6, runs QC, writes `bake/manifest/C5.json` and `C6.json`). Before that, once: `-m C5.refs` (refs in our frame), and `--with scipy -m C5.fit` (writes `bake/src/C5/params.json`).

## Method
- References are moved into OUR frame by `C5.refs`: x is stretched about 960 by k = our half gap / their half gap (gap = d/2 - R): 17 1.123, 04 1.158, 07 1.074. y is unchanged. Masks: bit 8 marks our dials (r<1.04 R) and the stretched 6WB dials, bit 4 the BMW telltales, and bit 16 the bar's text. Output goes to `bake/work/C5/ref/`.
- `C5/model.py` is a procedural model drawn in luma and coloured by a luma-to-RGB quadratic palette fitted on the 17 sky and floor. It has these parts:
  - **Outline:** top edge at y 79, |X| <= 240 (the box), 45 deg chamfers with r 10 joins, no side edges, and a clip at r 1.04 R around both dials.
  - **Background:** RGB keys at y 79/84/100/200/300/396 (luma 55 at the top, 45 at the horizon) and a faint top rim.
  - **Sky:** a ramp from y 398 to the horizon at 458 (power ~1.4, about +108 luma).
  - **Horizon:** a crisp 1x strip, a 2.5 px dark line with a bright lip.
  - **Floor:** 5 perspective light bars (y_k = y_v + 1/(u0 - k du)), a base ramp and two concave side walls. The floor has 29 numbers fitted by least squares; the luma rms is 10.4.
- No reference pixels are shipped. The photo only drives the fit.
