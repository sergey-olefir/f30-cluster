**Regenerate** (from `bake/src`):

```

UV_CACHE_DIR=../work/.uvcache uv run -q --no-project --with opencv-python-headless --with numpy --with pillow \
    --with scipy python -m C5.fit             # -> C5/params.json, bake/work/C5/redo/fit.json
./P9/py.sh -m C6.build --all                  # C5 + C6 layers, QC, bake/manifest/C5.json + C6.json
./P9/py.sh ../../tools/bake_scene.py          # repack apps/f30_cluster/hud/scene
./P9/py.sh -m C5.spread                       # per-reference key values (bake/work/C5/redo/spread.json)
./P9/py.sh -m C5.review <bench.png> <tag>     # review sheet vs the bench (bake/work/C5/redo/review_<tag>.png)
```

**No double correction.** The C5 and C6 layers are written in final panel values. `P10/levels.py` gives C5 and C6
the `identity` curve, and `P10.apply` copies their files unchanged (verified: md5 identical before / after
`P10.apply`). The order of `P10.apply` and the C5 / C6 builds therefore no longer matters. The C6 arc tracks carry
the colours P10's old window curve gave them (written into `C6/build.py`), so they are unchanged (mean within 0.1).

## Colour rule and targets (`C5/target.py`)

All 6WB references are phone photos of an LCD; their black is the LCD floor plus glare. Per reference:

1. Raw PANEL crop (no P0 step), x stretched into our frame about 960 (k = our half gap / theirs: 04 1.158,
   03 1.149, 02 1.139); y mapped so the glow's bottom edge → 458 and the front line → 608.8
   (04 463.3 / 615.4, 03 464.5 / 616.6, 06 466.5 / 618.6, 07 458 / 617.3, 17 457.5 / 608.6; 02 unmapped).
2. Local floor F: the median of the unlit sky plateau just above the glow (y 340–392, |X| < 150; 02: y 150–250).
   Below it, F grows linearly in linear light to the bar body's dark field (p20, y 622–642) by y 632. 04 and 06 carry
   a grey haze over the lower middle that runs into the bar; 03 and 02 have the bar black.
   F raw (sky / bar): 02 1/4/12 / 0/1/5 · 04 22/23/29 / 59/61/65 · 03 12/13/17 / 11/11/13 · 06 22/23/29 / 61/61/66 ·
   07 16/24/39 / 11/15/21 · 17 45/50/67 / 1/1/27.
3. L = (lin(raw) − lin(F)) / (lin(W) − lin(F)), W = P0's white numerals; panel = srgb(L · lin(fg)), with
   fg = (244, 246, 250), the theme's text white.

## What the middle is (04 / 03, confirmed by 02 / 06 / 07 / 17 for shape)

- **Sky:** black. The 02 render's sky equals its background; the photos' plateau is their floor. In 04 / 06 the
  top of the window brightens toward the top edge. That is a reflection on the lens (02 and 03 do not have it).
- **Sky glow:** flat across the window, from black at about y 400 rising (convex in code) to a peak just above the
  horizon. 04 49/55/58, 03 37/40/43, 02 42/54/83 (02's is longer, about 60 px). No separate bright lip in 03 / 04.
- **Horizon:** the glow's crisp bottom edge. Under it the floor is black (04 0, 03 about 8) down to the first row.
- **Floor:** 5 light rows ("stairs") at perspective positions, our frame 474 / 493 / 514 / 540 / 571 (the
  1 / (u0 − k du) law fits). Each row has a sharp bright top edge, then a body that decays toward the next row
  and ends in a thin dark gap. The first row is faint: 04 0/4/9, 03 14/16/19. The rows are lit from the centre and
  widen toward the front. Edge peaks (04 / 03): 23/31/36 / 26/29/32, 63/70/75 / 50/54/58, 107/127/129 / 81/95/96,
  152/174/179 / 130/138/147.
- **Walls:** a soft diagonal streak each side, X ≈ 170 + 1.23 (y − 500). They rise in from about y 500, peak around
  y 550–585 (04 75/95/96, 03 68/79/82, about 125 luma at y 585), and fade just above the front edge.
- **Front line:** 03 175/185/188, about 3.5 px FWHM, falling to half by |X| ≈ 160 and about 15 % at the ends. 02's
  line is 118/138/168. 04 / 06 show no line (no bar content).
- **Bar body:** black (03 6/7/8, 02 7/1/2). The old navy strip was LCD black and glare.
- **Hue:** photos, relative to their white numerals, give a cyan-grey. In linear R:G:B, floor 0.74 : 1.06 : 1.16
  and glow 0.79 : 1.04 : 1.19. The 02 render is much bluer (floor about 0.4 : 1 : 2.6). The photos are used.

## Model (`C5/model.py`, `params.json`, fitted by `C5/fit.py`)

The model is a sum of components in linear light, each with its own hue, re-encoded to sRGB. Values under half a
code are cut to exactly 0.

| part | params |
|---|---|
| glow | from 0 at y0 380.7 to 0.0305 at the horizon (458), power 3.33; no lip |
| rows | 5 at 253.1 + 1/(0.004519 − k·0.000344); peaks (linear) 0.0041 / 0.0164 / 0.0525 / 0.188 / 0.288; rise 2.5 px, body 0.72 of peak decaying over 0.61 of the spacing; lateral exp(−(X/h)^1.89), h = 61 + 107·t |
| walls | X = 170.2 + 1.226 (y − 500.6), σ 19 + 7.5 per 100 px, amplitude 0.0146 × (1 + 15.5 · Δy/100), in over 59 px, out 27 px before the front |
| line | y 608.8, σ 1.65 px, peak 0.414 (linear), ends exp(−(X/136.6)^2.93), floor 3 % |
| outline | unchanged: top 79, \|X\| ≤ 240, 45° chamfers, r 10, clip 1.04 R |

Fit RMS (code luma): glow 2.2, rows (centre) 7.7, floor 2D 8.2, line 16.6 (03's bar text sits right under it).
