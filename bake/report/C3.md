# P3: mini gauges C3 (fuel) and C4 (oil temperature), COMFORT

This report covers both C3 and C4 (`bake/report/C4.md` points here). The manifests are `bake/manifest/C3.json` and `C4.json`. The assets are in `apps/f30_cluster/hud/baked/C3/` and `C4/`.

Regenerate everything from `bake/src` (with `$PY` as in `bake/README.md`):

```sh
$PY -m C3.build && $PY -m C3.qc_run      # assets, merged images, manifests, then the fit numbers into the manifests
$PY -m C3.viewer                          # bake/work/C3/compare.png (17 | 04 | 07 | ours, both gauges)
uv run ... --with scipy python -m C3.fit  # optional re-tune (bake/work/C3/fit_log.json); the result is copied into build.P by hand
```

The measurement scripts are in `bake/src/C3/measure/` (unwraps, radial and angular profiles, the tick-scale fit).

How to read the numbers:

- **Crisp shapes** (ticks, arc, zones, bezel edge) meet the edge target (≤ 1.0 px) on 04 and 17 except: zones on 04 C3 (1.07), ticks on 04 C4 (1.14), and both pictograms on 17 (1.5). Their SSIM is low because the references blur 1.5–3 px features: 04 is upscaled 2.4×, and 17 and 07 have bloom. The fitter pushed widths up to match the blur, and I capped them (for example the arc at 0.030 R, not the 0.040 R the fitter preferred).
- **Soft layers**: the face meets ΔE ≤ 6 on 04 and 07 but stays below the SSIM target of 0.85. The reference noise floor sits on a flat field; the face has no structure to match.
- **Inner arc on 04 C4** fails (SSIM 0.01). The arc is almost invisible in that crop, and its eccentricity there is 0.018 R, not 0.046 R. The sources disagree, and I kept the majority.
- **Banding**: the merged images report `plateau_fraction` of about 0.3. This is a false positive. The face is a flat colour with no ramp, but qc.py treats the flat pixels next to thin features as "ramp" pixels. Every gradient in these layers is dithered.

Review sheets: `bake/work/C3/qc_<id>__<ref>.png` and `bake/work/C4/qc_<id>__<ref>.png`, for example `bake/work/C3/qc_fuel.merged__04_comfort_whole-cluster_mph.comfort.C3.png`. The overview is `bake/work/C3/compare.png`. The ROI masks are `bake/work/C?/roi_*`.

## Layers and proposed composition

Each gauge has 7 relightable element files, tightly cropped: face albedo @0.5x, bezel albedo, bezel highlight, inner arc, zones, ticks and pictogram. Together they come to 0.38 MiB (C3) and 0.42 MiB (C4) by w·h·8. They exist so the lead can relight or regenerate parts.

**Proposed for the scene: 1 image per gauge**, `C3/fuel.static.merged.png` at (8, 556) and `C4/oil.static.merged.png` at (1768, 556). Each is 144×144 RGBA at 1x, 165,888 bytes. That is **2 layers and 331,776 bytes (0.32 MiB)** in total. The needle stays dynamic on top, and the texts stay live. If live relighting of the bezel is wanted, split the bezel highlight back out as a third and fourth layer (+118,096 bytes each).

## Open questions and weak evidence

1. **Needle pivot**: is it at the bezel centre or at the scale centre (+0.046 R)? The 04 hub ring is ambiguous. If the tip must sit on the ticks, use the scale centre.
2. **Open bezel**: the 6WB mini bezel is open where the big dial's bezel passes. In the G20 layout the minis do not touch the main dials (9 px gap). Keep it as measured, or close the ring?
3. **Fuel "1/1"**: the top end shows two bars in 04, 17 and 02 ("11"). I treated it as live text and baked no tick at 1/1 (weak).
4. **Theme numbers** differ from the measurement: start, sweep and centre (above), and the zones (0.10 / 0.90 in the theme against 0.117 / 0.888 measured).
5. **Pictograms** are weak evidence. The 04 and 17 oil icon has an extra mark (':' and a line, maybe level or temperature). Ours is a plain can. P5 owns the canonical icon set (C7).
6. **Bezel light** is brightest away from the cluster centre in 04, flatter in 17, and too dim to read in 07. It does not support a single upper-left key light (`light.dir_deg` 235 on C3 and 305 on C4, in math degrees).
7. **References**: 07 C4 alignment is poor (edge error 4–5 px everywhere) despite P0 rating it "ok". 17's face levels are unusable because of haze. 02 (clean render) is a different variant: a coolant icon, a wave pictogram in the blue zone, a pump at the 0 end, different radii. I did not use it.
