## Light direction (measured, `bake/src/C1/light.py`, `bake/work/C1/light.json`)

Directions are in math degrees, in the speedo's frame. 05 is mirrored; in the rev dial's own frame a direction `a` becomes `180 − a`.
- **Bezel ridge brightness by angle**: 04 251°, 03 254°, 17 275° (weak, amplitude 0.23), 05 144° (mirrored), 02 82°, 12 340° (saturated). The profiles have two lobes:
  - top, at cw12 ≈ 8: 05 156–217, 04 145–223
  - lower outer side, at cw12 ≈ 232 for the speedo: 04 234, 05 229–234
  
  The lobes **mirror between the two dials**, so they are part of the display's drawing, not glass reflections.
  Modelled as base 0.26 + lobe (cw12 8°, σ 22°, gain 0.42) + lobe (232°, 32°, 0.72) + lobe (300°, 40°, 0.10).
- **Highlight position across the tube**: the radial centroid shift is under 0.004 R (about 1 px) at every angle, so it is noise. No direction can be read from it.
- **Groove inner rim brightness**: 05 → 127° in its own frame, 17 122°, 02 84°. That is a key light from the top / upper left.
  Adopted: `key_dir_deg = 115`, with ±35 % modulation on the rims and tube edges.
- The brief's "upper-left key plus a second light low right" does not describe the bezel in the photos. The second
  bright lobe is low on the **outer** side (lower left for the speedo).

No soft-layer banding: every layer is TPDF-dithered. The banding plateau the sheets report (about 0.28) comes from the flat face plateau over its full extent, which carries no ramp.

**Targets not met:**
- Crisp edge error is 2.7–4 px against a target of ≤ 1. The ticks are 2.9 px after roll compensation. Limits:
  - photo blur: the tick FWHM in 03 and 17 is 2–3× the real width
  - alignment: P0's ±1° roll is 5 px at the ticks
  - 03 decentring: the majors' angle error varies sinusoidally by ±1.2°
  
  The shapes are fitted in 13 @4x, where the widths agree to under 0.002 R.
- Soft layers reach SSIM ≥ 0.85 only for face (04), floor (05) and hub-well. The other soft layers are thin rings
  whose SSIM is noise-bound at luma 2–10.

## Open questions / weak evidence

1. Floor-shade (z 2.5) is **weak**. Below the floor line, 05 is darker (1–3 against 10) and 17 is at 0.7 of the face, but 04 and 03 show no darkening. It is kept at α 0.45, which gives a ratio of about 0.7, as in 17.
2. Hub-well is **weak**. No vignette is visible beyond the red hub glow (05, 15), so it is kept at α 0.30.
3. The ticks are measured partly on the rev dial (13 and 05, mirrored). The speedo's own close-up evidence (12) is a saturated variant.
4. The groove rim radius differs between refs: 05 0.795, 04 0.800, 03 0.810, 12 0.815. The value used, 0.807, is a compromise.
5. Light: there is no single key light. The model is a two-lobed bezel gloss (top plus lower outer) and a 115° key for the rims. P2 should use the mirror image for the rev dial: lobes at cw12 352 and 128, key at 65° math.
6. 02's proportions (disc 0.568, floor 0.134) were not followed. It was used for colour only.
